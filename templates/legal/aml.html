Compliance Standards: {{ site_settings.name|default:"Avingine" }} adheres to applicable Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations to prevent illicit financial activities and protect user funds.

1.0 Customer Due Diligence (KYC)

To verify player identities and adhere to statutory obligations:

  • Phone & Email Verification: Every player must register with a valid email address and an active mobile phone number registered under their name.
  • Identity Checks: Before processing high-volume withdrawal requests, we reserve the right to request proof of identity (e.g. valid national ID or passport) and proof of address.
  • Single Origin Account: Deposits and withdrawals must be made using payment accounts (such as M-Pesa phone numbers) registered in the player’s own name.

2.0 Transaction Monitoring

All ledger transactions are audited by automated fraud detection systems:

  • Depositing funds and requesting immediate withdrawal without active gameplay triggers an anti-money laundering review.
  • Operating multiple accounts to circumvent deposit or betting limits is strictly prohibited and grounds for immediate closure.
