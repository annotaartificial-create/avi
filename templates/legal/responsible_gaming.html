Underage Play is Strictly Prohibited

{{ site_settings.name|default:"Avingine" }} enforces age verification. Any account discovered to belong to someone under the age of 18 will be permanently closed.

1.0 Playing Responsibly

Multiplier games are designed strictly for entertainment. Never gamble with money you cannot afford to lose, and never view gaming as a way to earn an income or recover debts.

2.0 Self-Control Tools

Deposit Limits

Set daily, weekly, or monthly deposit ceilings. Increases require a mandatory cooling-off waiting period.

Take a Break

Temporarily pause your account from placing bets for 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days.

Self-Exclusion

Request complete self-exclusion for 6 months or permanently. All promotional outreach is blocked and remaining balances returned.

Reality Checks

View on-screen summaries of your elapsed session duration and net spend to stay in control.
